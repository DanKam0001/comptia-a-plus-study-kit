## C1P06-PBQ1: Set up Sam's print job

**Part A**

| Row | Setting | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Duplex | **On (both sides)** | She wants both sides of the paper |
| 2 | Orientation | **Landscape** | Wide spreadsheet, sheet turned sideways |
| 3 | Tray | **Tray 2 (A4)** | The A4 paper is in Tray 2 |
| 4 | Quality | **Draft** | Fast and uses less ink, fine for a meeting draft |
| 5 | Secure printing | **Held until sign-in** | Job is held until she signs in or taps her badge |
| 6 | Language | **PCL** | The everyday office language. PostScript is for graphics and professional publishing |

**Part B**

- **B1.** **Ethernet.** Shared on the network, the most reliable for a busy office.
- **B2.** **ADF.** The automatic document feeder pulls a stack through one page at a time.
- **B3.** **Flatbed.** A glass bed suits books and delicate paper.

**Marking (9 points):** 1 per row and 1 per quick choice.
- 8 to 9: strong. 6 to 7: fine, recheck the Configuration settings table. Under 6: reread the Deploy and Network scan sections.
- Common slip: row 3 as Tray 1. Read the paper size, not the tray order.

## C1P06-PBQ2: Match the maintenance and the feature

**Part A**

| Item | Letter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **C** Thermal | Clean the heating element (cool, soft cloth, isopropyl alcohol) |
| 2 | **A** Laser | A maintenance kit holds the replacement rollers and fuser |
| 3 | **D** Impact | Pins strike a ribbon, so it can print through multipart paper |
| 4 | **B** Inkjet | Clean the printheads, then calibrate |
| 5 | **C** Thermal | Faint thermal print means a dirty heating element |
| 6 | **A** Laser | The fuser is hot. Switch off and let it cool first |
| 7 | **B** Inkjet | Ink cartridges are inkjet |
| 8 | **D** Impact | The inked ribbon is replaced |

**Part B**

| Item | Feature | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Print server** | Manages the queue on the network and is always available |
| 2 | **Network scan to SMB** | Scan to a shared folder over SMB (port 445) |
| 3 | **Audit logs** | Records who printed or scanned what, and when |
| 4 | **Secured prints** | Job held until the owner signs in (PIN) |
| 5 | **Badging** | Tapping an ID badge on the printer |

Unused: ADF and Flatbed.

**Marking (13 points):** 1 per item.
- 12 to 13: strong. 9 to 11: fine, review the Maintenance table. Under 9: relearn the four printer types first.
