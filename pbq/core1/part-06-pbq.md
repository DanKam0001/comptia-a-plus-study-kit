## C1P06-PBQ1: Set up Sam's print job

- **Objective:** 3.7
- **Type:** Configure this (print settings form)
- **Time guide:** 5 minutes

### Scenario

Sam needs to print a wide 40-page spreadsheet. She wants it on both sides of the paper, with the sheet turned sideways so the columns fit. She wants to save ink because it is only a draft for a meeting. The A4 paper is loaded in Tray 2. The pages are confidential, so she wants them held until she taps her ID badge at the printer. It is an everyday office document, not a graphics or publishing job.

### Materials

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
|  PRINT SETTINGS                                           |
|-----------------------------------------------------------|
|  1. Duplex            ( Off / On - both sides )           |
|  2. Orientation       ( Portrait / Landscape )            |
|  3. Tray              ( Tray 1 Letter / Tray 2 A4 /       |
|                         Bypass tray - envelopes )         |
|  4. Quality           ( Draft / Normal / Best )           |
|  5. Secure printing   ( Off / Held until sign-in )        |
|  6. Language (driver) ( PCL / PostScript )                |
+-----------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Task

**Part A.** Write the setting you would choose for each of the six rows.

**Part B.** Three more quick choices for the same office:
- **B1.** Thirty busy users share one printer. Which connection is the most reliable? `USB`, `Ethernet` or `Wireless`.
- **B2.** Sam must scan a stack of 50 loose pages quickly. `ADF` or `Flatbed`?
- **B3.** She also has to scan one old bound book. `ADF` or `Flatbed`?

### Marking

9 points: 1 per row in Part A (6), 1 per quick choice in Part B (3).

## C1P06-PBQ2: Match the maintenance and the feature

- **Objective:** 3.7 and 3.8
- **Type:** Matching (two parts, letters can be reused in Part A)
- **Time guide:** 6 minutes

### Scenario

You look after four types of printer in a small office: a laser, an inkjet, a thermal receipt printer and an impact (dot matrix) printer. Part A gives you jobs and symptoms and you say which printer type each belongs to. Part B gives network needs and you pick the feature.

### Materials

**Part A: printer types.** `A` = Laser, `B` = Inkjet, `C` = Thermal, `D` = Impact. A letter can be used more than once.

1. Clean the heating element with a soft cloth and isopropyl alcohol, once it has cooled.
2. After a very high page count, apply a maintenance kit (new rollers and a fuser).
3. Prints three-layer carbon copy forms.
4. Streaky colour print: clean the printheads, then calibrate (align).
5. A shop receipt printer prints faintly. The special paper is fine. What needs cleaning?
6. Before cleaning inside, switch it off and wait for the fuser to cool.
7. Replace an ink cartridge, then align the printhead.
8. Replace the ribbon.

**Part B: features.** Choices: `Print server`, `Network scan to SMB`, `Audit logs`, `Secured prints`, `Badging`, `ADF`, `Flatbed`. Each is used at most once.

1. A printer is plugged into one PC that is often switched off, but everybody needs it any time.
2. Scan paperwork straight into a shared Windows folder.
3. A record of who printed or scanned what, and when.
4. A job waits until the owner signs in at the printer with a PIN.
5. Users sign in just by tapping an ID card on the printer.

### Task

Write a letter for each item in Part A and a feature for each item in Part B.

### Marking

13 points: 1 per item (8 in Part A, 5 in Part B).
