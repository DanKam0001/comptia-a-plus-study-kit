# Core 1, Part 16: PBQ answers

Do the tasks in [`part-16-pbq.md`](part-16-pbq.md) first.

**Marking note:** real PBQs can earn partial credit, so each item here is marked on its own. CompTIA does not publish its PBQ scoring rules, so treat this as practice marking, not the real formula. Rough guide: all correct is a pass for this drill, 75 percent or more means you are close, below 50 percent means re-read the [Part 16 cheatsheet](../../core1/part-16-exam-strategy-cram/cheatsheet.md).

## C1P16-PBQ1: Exam Clock Budget (8 points)

**Part A**

| # | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | 1 minute | 90 minutes for at most 90 questions is about one minute per question |
| A2 | 30 seconds | Stuck for about 30 seconds: pick your best answer, flag it, move on |
| A3 | 10 minutes | With about 10 minutes left, fill in anything still empty, because a blank can never score |

**Part B**

| # | Answer | Working |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | 20 minutes | 4 PBQs x 5 minutes |
| B2 | 10 minutes | 90 - 50 (multiple choice) - 20 (PBQs) - 10 (final check) = 10 |
| B3 | 40 minutes remaining | 90 - 50 = 40, so the PBQs start with 40 minutes left |
| B4 | 20 minutes remaining | 40 - 20 = 20, so the review starts with 20 minutes left |
| B5 | 10 minutes remaining | 20 - 10 = 10, so the final blank check starts with 10 minutes left, which matches A3 |

**Marking:** 1 point per blank. Accept "1", "one" and "about 1" for A1. For B3 to B5 the learner must give minutes remaining (40, 20, 10), not minutes elapsed (50, 70, 80).

## C1P16-PBQ2: Flag and Return Ordering (6 points)

| Order | Step | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | D Flag the unfamiliar PBQ and move on | PBQs take longer than multiple choice and often appear first, so flag and skip |
| 2 | B Answer the multiple choice, flagging if stuck about 30 seconds | Finish the multiple choice first so you bank those points |
| 3 | E Go back to the flagged PBQs | Come back with the time that is left, and a blank PBQ can never score (a plan rule in the task) |
| 4 | A Read the whole task, do what you know, fill in every part | Read the whole task before clicking, and never leave a PBQ blank |
| 5 | F Review flagged multiple choice with spare time | Spare time is for flagged questions, not for second-guessing answers you were sure of |
| 6 | C At about 10 minutes left, fill in anything blank | The final check always comes last, and a blank can never score |

**Answer string:** D, B, E, A, F, C

**Marking:** 1 point for each step in its correct position.
