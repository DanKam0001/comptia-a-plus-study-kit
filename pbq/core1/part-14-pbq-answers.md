# Core 1, Part 14: PBQ answers

Do the tasks in [`part-14-pbq.md`](part-14-pbq.md) first.

**Marking note:** real PBQs can earn partial credit, so each item here is marked on its own. CompTIA does not publish its PBQ scoring rules, so treat this as practice marking, not the real formula. Rough guide: all correct is a pass for this drill, 75 percent or more means you are close, below 50 percent means go back to the [cheatsheet](../../core1/part-14-troubleshooting-displays-mobile-printers/cheatsheet.md).

## C1P14-PBQ1: Read the Printouts (6 points)

| Sample | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | C | A line down every laser page is a dirty or damaged drum or toner cartridge |
| 2 | A | Garbled print means a wrong or corrupt driver |
| 3 | D | Faded prints mean low toner, toner-saving mode or a worn drum, and the fix is replacing or shaking the cartridge and turning off toner saver |
| 4 | E | Speckling is stray toner from a leaking cartridge or dirty printer |
| 5 | B | The page is cut off and the dialog shows Portrait, so change the orientation setting |
| 6 | F | Lines on an inkjet page mean a clogged print head, so run the head-cleaning cycle |

Unused fix: G (Restart the Print Spooler service). It fixes a stuck queue, which none of the samples shows.

**Answer string:** 1C 2A 3D 4E 5B 6F

**Marking:** 1 point per correct sample.

## C1P14-PBQ2: Ticket Queue (10 points)

| Ticket | Cause | Fix | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | 1 Burnt-out bulb | A | A projector with no picture and a high lamp-hours counter needs a new lamp (let it cool first) |
| T2 | 2 Overheating | B | Intermittent projector shutdown that clears after resting is overheating, so clean the filters and vents |
| T3 | 3 Swollen battery | C | A bulging battery is a fire risk: stop using it, do not charge it, replace it safely |
| T4 | 4 Digitizer fault | D | The touch layer is faulty while the picture is fine, so replace the screen and digitizer assembly |
| T5 | 5 Failing backlight or inverter | E | A faint picture at full brightness points to the backlight or inverter |

Unused: cause 6 (Incorrect input source) and fix F (Choose the right input). An input problem shows "No signal" or a black screen, not a faint picture.

**Marking:** 1 point per field (5 causes + 5 fixes). Mark the cause and the fix separately, so a right cause with a wrong fix still scores 1.
