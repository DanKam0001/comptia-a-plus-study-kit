# Core 2, Part 17: PBQ answers and marking

Questions are in `part-17-pbq.md`. One point per correct item, nothing for a wrong item. This kit marks each item separately. CompTIA does not publish its PBQ scoring rules, so treat this as practice marking, not the real formula.

---

## C2P17-PBQ1: Build the time budget (8 points)

**Part A (5 points)**

| # | Answer | Working |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 | 3 PBQs x 5 minutes |
| 2 | 15 | Given in the scenario (the kit's range is 10 to 15 minutes). All numbers here (3 PBQs, 5 minutes each, 87 questions) are invented for this exercise, not CompTIA figures |
| 3 | 60 | 90 - 15 - 15 |
| 4 | 41 | 60 minutes = 3600 seconds, 3600 / 87 = 41.4, rounded down to 41 |
| 5 | 75 | 90 - 15. Review starts when 15 minutes remain |

**Part B (3 points)**

| # | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | Do not sit on a hard question: choose your best answer, flag it, move on. |
| 2 | B | An unanswered question counts as wrong, so answer every question. |
| 3 | C | Do what you know, flag it, return after the multiple choice questions. |

Not used: **D** (blank is never better, because an unanswered question counts as wrong).

**Marking:** 8 = full marks. 6 to 7 = good. Below 6 = reread "Managing time" in the Part 17 cheat sheet. If line 4 is wrong by a few seconds, check your division. Only line 3 or line 5 wrong means a subtraction slip, not a strategy problem.

## C2P17-PBQ2: Flag and return in the right order (7 points)

| Your number | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | Check that no question is left blank. | Last check before you submit. |
| blank | Spend 20 minutes perfecting PBQ 1 before looking at any other question. | Bad idea: PBQs take longer, so bank time on the easy questions first. |
| 4 | Finish the flagged PBQ 1. | It is question 1, so it comes first in the flagged list. |
| 1 | Read PBQ 1, do the parts you know, flag it and move on. | Do what you know, flag it, return later. |
| 3 | At about 15 minutes left (every question already has an answer), open the flagged list. | Keep 10 to 15 minutes at the end for review. |
| 7 | Submit the exam. | Final step. |
| 5 | Review the remaining flagged multiple choice questions. | They come after PBQ 1 because the flagged list opens in question-number order. |
| 2 | Work through the multiple choice questions, give a best answer to every one, and flag the unsure ones. | Answer everything, nothing left blank. |

So the order is: PBQ read and flag (1), all multiple choice (2), open flagged list (3), finish PBQ 1 (4), review flagged multiple choice (5), blank check (6), submit (7).

**Marking:** 7 = full marks. 5 to 6 = good. Below 5 = reread "Performance-based questions" and "Managing time". Writing a number beside the 20-minute action costs you that point and usually shifts the others, so mark the others by their relative order if you did that.
