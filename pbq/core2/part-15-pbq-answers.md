# Core 2, Part 15: PBQ answers and marking

Questions are in `part-15-pbq.md`. One point per correct item, nothing for a wrong item. This kit marks each item separately. CompTIA does not publish its PBQ scoring rules, so treat this as practice marking, not the real formula.

---

## C2P15-PBQ1: Match the situation to the control (8 points)

| # | Letter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | A UPS is a battery that carries equipment through power loss long enough to shut down safely. |
| 2 | B | A surge suppressor blocks sudden spikes. |
| 3 | C | The MSDS covers handling and disposal. |
| 4 | D | Loose parts are stored and carried in antistatic bags. |
| 5 | E | An ESD strap drains static through the case instead of through the part. |
| 6 | F | Compressed air or a vacuum removes dust. |
| 7 | G | Batteries go to recycling or hazardous-waste collection, never normal rubbish. |
| 8 | H | Lift with your legs, keep your back straight, and get help with heavy items. |

Not used: **I** (batteries, toner and old devices do not go in the normal bin) and **J** (never use water on an electrical fire, and it is not a lifting or storage answer).

**Marking:** 8 = full marks. 6 to 7 = good. Below 6 = reread the 4.4 and 4.5 tables. Do not swap 1 and 2: the UPS is for power loss and under-voltage, the suppressor is for spikes.

## C2P15-PBQ2: Handle the customer and the incident (12 points)

**Part A (8 points)**

| # | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | DO | Restating the issue checks you understood it. |
| 2 | DON'T | Actively listen and avoid interrupting. |
| 3 | DO | Open-ended questions narrow the scope. |
| 4 | DON'T | Avoid distractions such as texting. |
| 5 | DON'T | Do not dismiss the customer's issue. |
| 6 | DO | If you will be late, contact the customer. |
| 7 | DO | Follow up later to verify satisfaction. |
| 8 | DON'T | Do not argue or be defensive. |

**Part B (4 points)**

| # | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | Chain of custody is the written record of who handled evidence. |
| 2 | B | Working from a copy preserves the original. |
| 3 | C | Order of volatility: most short-lived data first. |
| 4 | D | You inform management, and law enforcement as necessary, rather than investigating on your own. |

Not used: **E** (NDA, a promise to keep information secret) and **F** (AUP, the rules for using equipment and the network).

**Marking:** 12 = full marks. 9 to 11 = good. Below 9 = reread 4.6 and 4.7. In Part A, if a DON'T item was marked DO, picture the customer's face: that is the point of the question.
