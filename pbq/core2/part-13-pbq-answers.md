# Core 2, Part 13: PBQ answers and marking

Questions are in `part-13-pbq.md`. One point per correct item, nothing for a wrong item. This kit marks each item separately. CompTIA does not publish its PBQ scoring rules, so treat this as practice marking, not the real formula.

---

## C2P13-PBQ1: Match the phone problem to the first fix (8 points)

| # | Letter | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | A stale pairing is the usual cause. Forget the device and pair again. |
| 2 | B | Airplane mode switches off Wi-Fi. |
| 3 | C | NFC works over only a few centimetres, and a thick case can block it. |
| 4 | D | Rotation lock stops the screen turning. |
| 5 | E | Low storage is a common reason an OS update fails. |
| 6 | F | Check battery usage by app first, then brightness and background apps. |
| 7 | G | The easy fix ladder for a crashing app: force close, restart, update, reinstall. |
| 8 | H | A stale saved network is fixed by forgetting it and rejoining. |

Not used: **I** (rooting removes protections and is a security risk) and **J** (apps from outside the official store are not checked).

**Marking:** 8 = full marks. 6 to 7 = good. Below 6 = reread the 3.2 table. Choosing I or J means you applied a security risk as a fix.

## C2P13-PBQ2: Triage the security problems (12 points)

**Part A (8 points: 1 for each cause, 1 for each action)**

| Ticket | Cause | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A, Ransomware | 1 | Renamed, unopenable files plus a money demand is ransomware. Isolate, do not pay, restore from backup. |
| 2 | B, Rogue antivirus | 2 | Real antivirus does not ask for a phone call or payment in a pop-up. |
| 3 | C, Malicious extension or changed proxy or DNS | 3 | Redirection and constant pop-ups point to a bad extension or changed settings. |
| 4 | D, Sideloaded malicious app | 4 | Battery drain and ads after an app from outside the official store. Remove it, scan, use only the official store. |

Not used in Part A: cause **E** and **F**, and actions **5** and **6**. Developer mode should be off unless needed.

**Part B (4 points)**

| # | Answer |
|---|---|
| 1 | P, Sideloading |
| 2 | Q, Jailbreaking |
| 3 | R, Rooting |
| 4 | S, Application spoofing |

Not used: **T** (developer mode unlocks powerful settings for app makers and should stay off unless needed).

**Marking:** 12 = full marks. 9 to 11 = good. Below 9 = reread the 3.3 and 3.4 tables. A very common slip is swapping jailbreaking and rooting: jailbreaking is iPhone, rooting is Android.
