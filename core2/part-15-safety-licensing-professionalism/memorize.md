# Memorise list: Safety, Environment, Licensing, Privacy and Professionalism (Core 2, Part 15)

Understand the video first, then learn these cold. They're the facts a scenario question assumes you already know.
Flashcards: [`flashcards/core2_part15.csv`](../../flashcards/core2_part15.csv) (import into Anki or any flashcard app).

## Safety

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does ESD stand for? | Electrostatic Discharge |
| First step before repairing a PC | Disconnect the power |
| Where does an ESD strap clip? | Bare metal on the case |
| What do you store loose parts in? | Antistatic bags |
| Safe way to lift | Bend the knees, lift with your legs, keep your back straight |
| Fire type to avoid water on | Electrical fires |
| Goggles protect, mask protects | Goggles protect your eyes. An air filter mask protects your lungs from dust |

## Environment

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does MSDS stand for? | Material Safety Data Sheet |
| What does an MSDS tell you? | How to handle and dispose of a product safely |
| Batteries and toner go where? | Recycling or hazardous waste, never normal rubbish |
| Air too dry causes | Static |
| Air too humid causes | Condensation and corrosion |
| How to remove dust | Compressed air or a vacuum |
| Surge suppressor vs UPS | Suppressor blocks spikes. UPS is a battery for under-voltage events and power loss |

## Incident response, licensing and policy

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Chain of custody | A written record of who handled evidence, and when |
| Why work from a copy of the drive? | Preserves the original and its data integrity |
| Order of volatility | Most short-lived data first (memory before disk) |
| Who do you tell about prohibited content? | Management, and law enforcement as necessary |
| DRM / EULA | Digital Rights Management / End-User License Agreement |
| Perpetual license | Bought once and kept |
| Personal vs corporate license | A personal license can't be used for business |
| NDA / MNDA | Non-Disclosure Agreement / mutual NDA (both sides bound) |
| Four kinds of regulated data | Credit card information, government-issued ID, PII, healthcare data |
| PII stands for | Personally Identifiable Information |
| AUP stands for | Acceptable Use Policy |
| Where are compliance rules often shown? | Splash screens |

## Professionalism

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Attire | Match the environment: formal or business casual |
| Language | No jargon, acronyms or slang |
| If you will be late | Contact the customer |
| Things to avoid on the job | Personal calls, texting, social media |
| Never do to a difficult customer | Argue, be defensive, dismiss, judge |
| How to clarify | Open-ended questions, restate the issue, verify understanding |
| After the job | Provide documentation, follow up later |
