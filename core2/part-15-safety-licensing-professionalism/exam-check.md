# Exam check: Safety, Environment, Licensing, Privacy and Professionalism

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A technician is about to replace a part inside a desktop PC that is still plugged into the wall. What should the technician do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Disconnect the power and wear an ESD strap. Always disconnect power before repairing a PC, and the strap protects parts from static.</details>

**2.** A technician finds prohibited content on an employee's work computer. What should the technician do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Report it to management and preserve the evidence. Leave it untouched, keep a chain of custody and document everything.</details>

**3.** A customer is angry, and shouts that their computer is useless. How should the technician respond?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Stay calm, listen without interrupting, and restate the problem. Don't argue or be defensive.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Where do you find out how to dispose of a chemical product safely?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The material safety data sheet (MSDS).</details>

**5.** A site suffers brief power dips that keep rebooting PCs. What protects against this?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A UPS. (A surge suppressor only blocks spikes.)</details>

**6.** Which kind of license lets you use software for a company's staff, not just yourself?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A corporate-use license. A personal-use license can't be used for business.</details>

**7.** Two companies need to share secret plans with each other. Which agreement fits?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A mutual non-disclosure agreement (MNDA).</details>

**8.** A spreadsheet holds customers' names with their home addresses. What kind of data is this?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>PII (personally identifiable information), because it identifies individual people. It needs careful handling.</details>

**9.** In an investigation, which data do you collect first: the hard drive or the contents of memory?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Memory. The order of volatility says to collect the most short-lived data first.</details>

**10.** A technician will be 20 minutes late to a customer's office. What should they do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Contact the customer.</details>
