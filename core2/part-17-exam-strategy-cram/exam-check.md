# Exam check: Exam Strategy, PBQs and a Cram Review

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A performance-based question appears that you don't recognise, with a screen full of settings. What should you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Do the parts you know, flag it, and come back later. It shouldn't eat the time you need for the rest of the exam.</details>

**2.** With ten minutes left, you still have many questions unanswered. What should you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Answer every remaining question, guessing where you must. An unanswered question counts as wrong, so a guess can only help.</details>

**3.** Two answers both look correct. What is the best way to choose?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Re-read the question for words like first, best or most likely, then match your answer to that exact situation.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** What score do you need to pass Core 2?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>700 on a scale of 100 to 900.</details>

**5.** Which two domains are each worth 28 percent of Core 2?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Operating Systems and Security.</details>

**6.** In the malware removal procedure, what comes straight after investigating and verifying the symptoms?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Quarantine the infected system.</details>

**7.** Which backup type needs the last full backup plus every later backup to restore?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Incremental.</details>

**8.** About how long can you spend on each question on average?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>About one minute (90 questions in 90 minutes), less on easy ones so there is time for PBQs.</details>
