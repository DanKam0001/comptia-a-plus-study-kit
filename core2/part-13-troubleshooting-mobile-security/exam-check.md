# Exam check: Troubleshooting Mobile OS, Apps and PC Security Issues

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A phone's battery drains fast and it's full of ads. The user installed a free app from a website outside the official store. What is the most likely cause?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A malicious sideloaded app. Why: apps outside the official store aren't checked. Remove it, scan, and only install from the official store.</details>

**2.** A user's browser keeps redirecting their searches to a strange website, and pop-ups keep appearing. What should you do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Remove unknown extensions, check the proxy and DNS settings, and scan for malware. Why: redirection and random pop-ups are browser symptoms of adware or a bad extension.</details>

**3.** A phone won't connect to a Bluetooth speaker that worked yesterday. What is the best first step?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Forget the speaker, then pair it again. Why: pairing can go stale. Also check Bluetooth is on and the speaker is close.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** What is the name for a fake app that imitates a real one?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Application spoofing.</details>

**5.** A user's files have been renamed and can't be opened. What is this typical of, and what do you do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Ransomware. Isolate the PC from the network, then follow the malware removal procedure and restore from backup.</details>

**6.** A pop-up says "Your antivirus found 100 threats. Call this number." What is it?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A false alert from rogue antivirus (scareware).</details>

**7.** A phone's screen stays upright when it is turned sideways. What do you check?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Whether rotation lock (auto-rotate off) is on.</details>

**8.** A phone fails to install an OS update. Name three common causes.
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Low storage, low battery, poor connection.</details>
