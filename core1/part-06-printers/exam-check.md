# Exam check: Printers and Multifunction Devices

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** Ten people in an office share one printer, but it's plugged into a single PC that is often switched off, so nobody else can print. What is the best fix?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Connect the printer to the network and use a print server. A shared printer on one PC only works while that PC is on.</details>

**2.** A business needs to print invoices on carbon copy paper with three layers, all in one go. Which type of printer should it use?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>An impact printer. Only its pins strike hard enough to mark every layer of multipart paper.</details>

**3.** Confidential payslips sit in the printer's output tray where anyone walking past can read them. Which feature fixes this?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Secured print, released with a PIN or badge. The job only prints when its owner signs in at the printer.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which language is used by printers for graphics and professional publishing, and who made it?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>PostScript, made by Adobe. PCL is the common everyday language.</details>

**5.** A user needs to scan a stack of 40 pages into one file quickly. Which scanner feature do you use?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The ADF (automatic document feeder).</details>

**6.** A user needs to scan a bound book. ADF or flatbed?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Flatbed. The ADF is for loose sheets.</details>

**7.** An inkjet prints streaky lines but the cartridges are full. What two maintenance steps come first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Clean the printheads, then calibrate (align).</details>

**8.** A shop's receipt printer prints faint receipts. Which part do you clean?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The heating element (let it cool first). Also check the thermal paper.</details>

**9.** Which part of a laser printer melts toner onto the page, and what must you do before opening the printer?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The fuser. Switch the printer off and let it cool.</details>
