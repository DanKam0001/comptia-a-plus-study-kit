# Exam check: Laptops and Mobile Devices

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A technician replaces the Wi-Fi card inside a laptop, but afterward the signal is very weak. What was most likely missed?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The antenna wires were not connected back onto the card. They snap on and run up through the hinge to the screen.</details>

**2.** A company wants every staff phone to use a passcode, and wants to wipe any phone that gets lost. What does this?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Mobile device management (MDM). It enforces policies and can lock or wipe a phone remotely.</details>

**3.** A user turns on a Bluetooth headset, but the phone can't find it in the list. What should they do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Put the headset into pairing mode. Bluetooth devices must be discoverable before they can be found.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which memory form factor does a laptop normally use?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>SODIMM.</details>

**5.** What is the difference between a docking station and a port replicator?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A dock gives one connection to a screen, keyboard, network and often charging, and can add extra ports and features. A port replicator only repeats the ports the laptop already has.</details>

**6.** Which connector is small, oval and works either way up?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>USB-C (24 pins, reversible).</details>

**7.** Which location method works best outdoors, and which still works indoors?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>GPS (satellites) is best outdoors. Cellular location (towers) still works indoors, with less accuracy.</details>

**8.** An employee uses their own phone for work. What is this called?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>BYOD, bring your own device.</details>
