# Lab: Audit a phone and a laptop (no tools needed)

**Time:** 20 minutes. **Needs:** your phone (Android or iPhone) and a laptop. Nothing here changes settings unless you choose to.

## 1. Phone network settings

Open **Settings** on your phone and find each of these. Write down whether it is on or off:
- Wi-Fi and the network you are on
- Mobile/cellular data, and the network type shown in the status bar (4G or 5G)
- Hotspot / tethering
- Airplane mode
- SIM and **eSIM** (look under SIM or Cellular settings: do you have a physical SIM, an eSIM, or both?)
- Data usage: is there a data warning or limit set?

## 2. Bluetooth

Pair (or look at the pair list for) a Bluetooth device. Name the five steps as you do it: enable Bluetooth, enable pairing, find the device, enter the PIN if asked, test it.

## 3. Location services

Find **Location** in settings. Which apps can use it? Does the phone use GPS, Wi-Fi and cellular? Turn off location for one app that does not need it.

## 4. Sync

Find your account settings. Which of these sync: calendar, contacts, mail, cloud storage? Is a data cap or "Wi-Fi only" setting on?

## 5. The laptop

Look up your laptop's exact model and find its **service manual** (search "<model> service manual"). Find:
- Where the battery, RAM and drive are. Is the RAM SODIMM or soldered?
- Is the drive 2.5-inch or M.2?
- Where the Wi-Fi card and its antenna leads are.

Look only. Do not open the laptop unless you know how, and always shut down, unplug and disconnect the battery first.

## 6. Port inventory

List every port on your laptop and phone. Which are USB-C, USB-A, microUSB, Lightning? Which would a **port replicator** copy, and what extras would a **docking station** add?

## Check yourself

- A headset won't appear in the Bluetooth list. What do you check?
- A company wants a passcode and remote wipe on every staff phone. Which tool?
- After replacing a laptop Wi-Fi card, the signal is poor. What was missed?
