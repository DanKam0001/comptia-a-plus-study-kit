# Lab: Rehearse exam day (free, no special equipment)

**Time:** about 90 minutes for the timed run, plus 20 minutes to review. **Needs:** the free practice exam in this kit ([`exams/core1`](../../exams/core1/practice-exam-1-questions.md)), a timer (your phone is fine), and a pen and paper.

Goal: practice the technique from Part 16 until it is automatic, so the real exam feels familiar.

## 1. Set the conditions

1. Open the practice exam questions. Do **not** open the answer key.
2. Set a timer for **90 minutes**, the same as the real exam. Keep your phone away from the questions.
3. On paper, make two columns: **Flagged** and **Guessed**.

## 2. Run it with the technique

1. Go through the paper fast. About a minute per question.
2. If you are stuck for about 30 seconds, write your best answer, add the question number to **Flagged**, and move on.
3. Look for the key words **FIRST**, **BEST**, **MOST LIKELY**, **NOT**. Underline them.
4. If you have a question with two good answers, cross out the plainly wrong ones, then pick the simple, low-risk step that fits the exact situation.
5. With 5 minutes left, answer every blank. A blank can never score.

## 3. Mark it

1. Open the answer key and mark your paper.
2. For every wrong answer, write the **objective number**. The key tells you which one.
3. Count: how many wrong answers were in **Flagged**? Were they mostly guesses? That shows what you did not know, rather than what you misread.

## 4. Turn it into a plan

Make a table:

```
Objective   Wrong answers   Which part to re-watch   Flashcards to review
```

Go to those parts first. Run their flashcards daily for the next week.

## 5. Practice a PBQ yourself

PBQs test doing, not recalling. Do three small real tasks and time yourself:

- **Match connectors:** gather any cables you own (USB, HDMI, Ethernet, power) and name each one and what it connects to.
- **Order the steps:** write the steps to replace a laptop battery safely, in order, from memory. Then check them against Part 11.
- **Set it up:** in Windows, open **Settings > Network & internet** and find the IP address, the Wi-Fi band and the Wi-Fi security type. Time yourself.

## Check yourself

- What do you do when you open a PBQ you do not recognise?
- How long do you give a question before you guess and flag it?
- Two answers look right, and the question says "first". What do you choose?

## Going further (optional)

Take the practice exam a second time a week later, using the same conditions. The score trend matters more than any single result.
