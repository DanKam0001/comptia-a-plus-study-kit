# Exam check: Core 1 Exam Strategy, PBQs and a Cram Review

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** You open a performance-based question you don't recognise, with a simulated screen of settings, and you have no idea where to start. What is the best approach?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Flag it, finish the multiple choice, then come back and do the parts you know.<br><br><em>Why:</em> PBQs take longer. Banking the multiple choice points first is safer, and a PBQ should never be left blank.</details>

**2.** With ten minutes left on the clock, you still have twenty questions unanswered. What should you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Answer every one, guessing where you must.<br><br><em>Why:</em> a blank question scores nothing, so a guess can only help.</details>

**3.** Two answers both look right, and the question asks what you should do first. Which should you choose?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The simple, low-risk step that fits the situation.<br><br><em>Why:</em> "first" questions usually reward the cheap, easy step over a big repair. Re-read the key word to be sure.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** What passing score do you need on Core 1, and on what scale?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>675 on a scale of 100 to 900.</details>

**5.** Which Core 1 domain carries the most weight?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Hardware and Network Troubleshooting, 28%.</details>

**6.** You fail Core 1 on your first attempt. When can you retake it?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Straight away. The 14 day wait applies from the second failure on.</details>

**7.** What ID do you need on exam day?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Two valid IDs, with names matching your booking exactly. One must be government-issued with a photo and signature.</details>

**8.** About how long should you spend on each question?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>About one minute. If stuck for about 30 seconds, guess, flag, and move on.</details>

**9.** Is the six-step troubleshooting method tested on 220-1201?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>No. It is useful as a work habit, but it is not an exam objective.</details>

**10.** Which key words should you underline in a question?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>FIRST, BEST, MOST LIKELY, NOT and LEAST.</details>
