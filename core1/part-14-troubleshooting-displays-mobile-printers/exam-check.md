# Exam check: Troubleshooting Displays, Mobile Devices and Printers

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A projector shuts itself off halfway through a presentation, then works again after it cools down. What is the most likely cause?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Blocked filters and vents are making it overheat. Clean them and leave space for airflow.<br><br><em>Why:</em> a projector that recovers after resting is overheating. A burnt-out lamp would give no picture at all.</details>

**2.** A customer's phone battery has puffed up, and the back of the phone is lifting off. What should the technician do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Stop using it, don't charge it, and replace the battery safely.<br><br><em>Why:</em> a swollen battery is a fire risk. Never charge, press or puncture it, and recycle the old one properly.</details>

**3.** A laser printer puts the same dark line down every page it prints. What is the most likely cause?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A dirty or damaged drum or toner cartridge.<br><br><em>Why:</em> a mark in the same place on every page comes from a part that touches every page.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** A monitor shows "No signal" but the PC is on and the cable is firmly plugged in. What do you check first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The monitor's input source setting.</details>

**5.** A screen is crisp, but touches are ignored, or the screen taps by itself. Which part is likely at fault?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The digitizer.</details>

**6.** Printed pages show a faint copy of the previous image (an echo). Which parts are the likely cause?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A worn drum, or the fuser.</details>

**7.** A user's printer queue shows five stuck jobs and nothing prints. What do you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Clear the queue and restart the Print Spooler service.</details>

**8.** A phone cannot install a new app. Which two causes do you check first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Free storage, and whether the operating system is up to date.</details>

**9.** A projected picture is very dim, and the lamp hours counter shows the lamp is old. What is the fix?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Replace the projector lamp (let it cool first), and clean the filter.</details>

**10.** A laser printer makes a grinding noise and the paper will not feed. What should you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Stop, power off, and look inside for a foreign object or broken gear. Replace worn rollers or gears.</details>
