# Exam check: Troubleshooting Motherboards, RAM, CPU, Power, Drives and RAID

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A PC makes a pattern of beeps when it is switched on, and nothing appears on the screen. What should the technician do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Look up the beep pattern in the motherboard manual. Beep codes differ by maker and show which part failed the power-on self-test.</details>

**2.** A PC keeps forgetting the date and time whenever it is unplugged. What is the most likely fix?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Replace the CMOS battery, a small coin cell on the motherboard.</details>

**3.** A server's RAID 5 array sounds an alarm and one drive shows an amber light. What should the technician do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Replace the failed drive and let the array rebuild. RAID 5 survives one failed drive but is degraded until rebuilt.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** A customer's PC smells of burning. What is the first action?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Power it off and unplug it at once. Do not power it on again until the part is found and replaced.</details>

**5.** A hard drive makes a clicking sound. What should you do first?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Back up the data immediately, then replace the drive.</details>

**6.** A PC shuts down by itself after a few minutes of heavy use, and the fan is clogged with dust. What is the likely cause and fix?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Overheating. Clean the dust out, check the fans spin, and replace the thermal paste if needed.</details>

**7.** The screen says "bootable device not found". What three things do you check?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The boot order in the firmware, the drive's cables, and whether the drive is detected in the firmware at all.</details>

**8.** A S.M.A.R.T. warning appears for a drive. What does it mean?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The drive predicts it will fail soon. Back up now and replace the drive.</details>
