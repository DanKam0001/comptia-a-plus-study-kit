# Exam check: Displays

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A designer complains that the colors on their monitor look washed out when viewed from the side. Which choice fixes this?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>IPS. It keeps accurate color across wide viewing angles. TN fades from the side.</details>

**2.** A gamer says fast action looks choppy on their 60 hertz monitor. What should they look for?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A higher refresh rate, such as 144 Hz, with a graphics card, cable and settings that support it.</details>

**3.** An older laptop screen is very dim, but shining a flashlight on it shows the picture is still there. Which part has most likely failed?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The inverter, which powers the fluorescent backlight tube.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which display type has no backlight at all and can show perfect black?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>OLED. Each pixel makes its own light and can switch fully off.</details>

**5.** What is the resolution of 4K UHD?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>3840 x 2160.</details>

**6.** Two screens have the same resolution, one 24 inches and one 32 inches. Which has the higher pixel density?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The 24-inch. The same pixels are packed into a smaller area.</details>

**7.** A tablet shows a clear picture but ignores all touches. Which component is suspect?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The digitizer.</details>

**8.** Which LCD type is known for deep blacks and high contrast but slower response?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>VA.</details>
