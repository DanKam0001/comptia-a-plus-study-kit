# Exam check: Power Supplies, Cables and Connectors

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A desktop PC dies the moment it's plugged in, because the power supply's voltage switch is set for the wrong country. What should be set before plugging it in?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The input voltage switch, to match local power (110-120 V or 220-240 V). A wrong setting can destroy the supply.</details>

**2.** An office needs ten gigabit Ethernet over a ninety meter cable run, but the cable they bought can't deliver it. Which cable category should they use?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Category 6a. Cat 5e is only 1 Gbps, and plain Cat 6 reaches 10 Gbps only to about 55 m.</details>

**3.** A laptop has only USB-C ports, but the projector only has an HDMI port, and no picture shows. What is needed?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A USB-C to HDMI cable or adapter. USB-C can carry video in DisplayPort alternate mode.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which cable type is required in the air space above a ceiling?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Plenum-rated cable (fire-resistant, low-smoke jacket).</details>

**5.** How do T568A and T568B differ, and what does A on one end and B on the other make?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>They swap the green and orange pairs. A to B makes a crossover cable.</details>

**6.** Which fiber type uses a laser and reaches kilometers?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Single-mode fiber (about 9 micron core).</details>

**7.** A server must keep running if one power supply fails. What kind of PSU setup is that?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Redundant: two supplies in one chassis.</details>

**8.** What is the speed of USB 3.0, and of Thunderbolt 3 and 4?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>USB 3.0 = 5 Gbps. Thunderbolt 3 and 4 = 40 Gbps (over USB-C).</details>
