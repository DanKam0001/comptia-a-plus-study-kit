# Exam check: Power Supplies, Cables and Connectors

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** Which cable type is required in air-handling spaces above ceilings?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Plenum-rated cable.</details>

**2.** How do T568A and T568B differ, and what does A on one end and B on the other make?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>They swap the green and orange pairs. A to B makes a crossover cable.</details>

**3.** A technician needs 10 Gbps Ethernet over a 90 m run. Which category?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Cat 6a.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which power supply output rail powers the CPU and graphics card?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The 12 V rail.</details>

**5.** What is the 24-pin motherboard power connector made of?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A 20-pin plug plus a detachable 4-pin piece (20+4).</details>

**6.** Which fiber connector is small and has an RJ45-style latch?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>LC (Lucent Connector).</details>

**7.** Which video cable is analog, 15-pin and blue?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>VGA.</details>

**8.** A projector only has a DVI-D input and the laptop only has USB-C. What do you need?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>A USB-C to DVI adapter or cable that supports video (DisplayPort alternate mode).</details>
