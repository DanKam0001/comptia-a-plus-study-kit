# Exam check: Wireless and Setting Up a SOHO Network

Cover the answers and try each one out loud first.

**1.** A customer's Wi-Fi is slow every evening. Their router uses the crowded 2.4 gigahertz band, like all their neighbors. What should you do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>Move the router to the 5 gigahertz band or a clear channel (1, 6 or 11). Crowded channels cause interference.</details>

**2.** A laptop connects to the Wi-Fi but can't reach the internet. Its IP address starts with 169.254. What does this tell you?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>It could not get an address from DHCP, so it gave itself an APIPA address. Local network only, no internet.</details>

**3.** A customer wants to pay by tapping their phone on a card reader. Which wireless technology makes that work?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>NFC. It works only within a few centimetres, which is why you tap.</details>

## More practice (written for this kit)

**4.** Which 2.4 GHz channels do not overlap in the US?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>1, 6 and 11.</details>

**5.** Which 802.11 standard works only on 5 GHz?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>802.11ac (Wi-Fi 5). 802.11a also uses only 5 GHz, but it is the older one.</details>

**6.** Which range is a private IPv4 address: 172.15.4.2, 172.20.4.2 or 169.254.4.2?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>172.20.4.2. The private range is 172.16.0.0 to 172.31.255.255. 169.254 is APIPA.</details>

**7.** On a new SOHO router, which port takes the internet line?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>The WAN port. Computers go in the LAN ports.</details>

**8.** What does the default gateway do?
<details><summary>Answer</summary>It is the router address that traffic goes to when the destination is not on the local network.</details>
